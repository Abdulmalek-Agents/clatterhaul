# Clatterhaul — Marketing & Steam Page Plan

**Version:** 2.0  ·  **Owner:** Market & Community Critics + Creative Director  ·  Source of R1-MKT-10 / R3-MKT-9 resolution

> With 60+ games launching daily, **discovery is the #1 risk** and **review score + clips are the currency.** The whole game is engineered to be clip-able; marketing converts those clips into wishlists.

## 1. Positioning
- **One-liner:** "Build a terrible machine. Haul it home. Try not to die laughing."
- **Comps for shoppers:** "PEAK meets Human Fall Flat with a precious, screaming cargo."
- **Pillars on the page:** 2–4p co-op, physics chaos, proximity voice, build-your-rig, ~10h+ of hauls, sub-$13.

## 2. Steam page
- **Capsule:** A/B test 2–3 capsules; hero reads as *chaotic co-op physics* at thumbnail size (silhouette of an absurd rig mid-disaster + 4 panicking crew).
- **Short description:** punchy, comps-forward, mentions co-op 2–4 + proximity voice + Steam Deck.
- **Tags:** Co-op, Multiplayer, Physics, Funny, Comedy, Online Co-Op, Local Co-Op (Remote Play), Casual, Family Friendly.
- **6 hero GIFs/videos:** (1) catastrophic tip in slow-mo, (2) winch save, (3) cargo reaction gag, (4) Boss-Haul triumph, (5) Garage build montage, (6) Blooper Reel.
- **Trailer beats (45–60s):** hook disaster (3s) → the pitch → 3 escalating biome gags → build-a-rig → triumphant Boss-Haul → logo + "Wishlist now."

## 3. Wishlist funnel ("clip-to-wishlist" loop)
In-game one-click clip export → players post to TikTok/Reddit/X/Discord → viewers hit Steam page → hero GIFs + demo → wishlist → launch/Next-Fest notification → buy + clip. The product *is* the ad (docs 10, 11).

## 4. Beats / calendar (no dates — sequence)
1. Steam page up early (capsule + GIFs) to accrue wishlists.
2. Community: Discord + Reddit (r/CoOpGaming etc.) devlogs + clip drops.
3. **Steam Next Fest demo** — the single most important beat: a tight, hyper-clip-able slice (Canyon + one Calamity + Boss-Haul) with a wishlist CTA.
4. Creator keys + clip contest pre-launch.
5. Launch at a sale-adjacent window; "Better With Friends"-style messaging; friend-pass consideration.
6. Post-launch seasons (doc 17) sustain visibility.

## 5. Creator/streamer strategy
High clip-density + proximity voice = streamer catnip (the PEAK/R.E.P.O. playbook). Provide keys, a press kit, seed-sharing ("play this exact disaster"), and a clip contest. Optimize audio loudness for broadcast (doc 14).

## 6. Pricing & regional
$9.99–$12.99 global; tuned regional pricing for LATAM/SEA/EE to maximize group-buys; launch discount to spike the algorithm; friend-pass trial leg to lower group friction.

## 7. Localization for reach (doc 07)
Text loc by wishlist geography (FR/DE/ES-LATAM/PT-BR/zh-Hans/JA); visual-first comedy travels.

## 8. KPIs
Wishlists (pre-launch velocity), demo → wishlist conversion, review score @ launch (>90% gate), clip volume, creator coverage, CCU curve. Review score is the north star — protect it (docs 06, 16).
