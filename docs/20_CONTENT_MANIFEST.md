**Version:** 3.1 (dev-ready)  ·  **Owner:** Level + Systems Designers  ·  Entry point: `00_PRODUCTION_BIBLE.md`

## 7. Endless — "The Long Haul" (infinite replay)
- Procedurally **chains legs** drawn from the 22-contract pool (biome-shuffled), re-themed with fresh start/exit points.
- **Escalation:** every 2 legs adds **+1 mutator** from the deck (doc 23) until the ×2.5 cap.
- **Scoring:** `Σ(leg payout × (1 + Σ mutator ×))`; personal + friends bests tracked (Steam Leaderboards).
- **Forgiving fail:** payload destroyed or crew wiped → run banks score so far. Encourages "push your luck."
- Targets the +4–6h "reason to return" budget per doc 09.

## 8. Daily Gauntlet (the daily hook)
- One **UTC-date seed** picks 1 biome leg + 2–3 mutators for **everyone that day** (deterministic in Core, doc 02).
- Global + friends leaderboards; **cosmetic-only** rewards (frames/shards) — no power, low cheat incentive.
- ~10–15 min/run; the social "did you beat today's?" loop.

## 9. Free Play / Workshop (creative longevity)
- Pick any biome + mutators + cargo; sandbox.
- **Workshop:** publish/subscribe **contraption blueprints**; "Foreman's Picks" weekly (doc 17). Effectively infinite UGC content.

## 10. Mastery layer (completionist 10h+)
- **Perfect Hauls:** per-contract S-tier (0% loss + ≤par + no module lost) → trophy cosmetics (34 to chase).
- **20 Union Ranks + Prestige** (doc 12) — steady cosmetic drip.
- **Foreman's Challenges:** 3 daily + 3 weekly.
- **~40 funny Steam achievements.**

## 11. Content totals (scope tracking)
- **34 hand-tuned hauls** (22 Contracts + 6 Boss + 6 Calamity) + Endless/Daily generators + Workshop UGC.
- 40 modules (21) · 12 cargo (22) · 16 mutators (23) · 60+ cosmetics · 8 emotes · 120+ barks (24).
- **Time-to-content:** ~6.5h first clear + 4–6h Endless/Daily/mastery + ongoing UGC/seasons → **≥10h engaged play, effectively unbounded.**
- All reuse one physics loop + asset-store art → high content-per-dollar.
