# Clatterhaul — Accessibility

**Version:** 2.0  ·  **Owner:** Accessibility Specialist (with UX, Audio, Design)  ·  Source of R2-ACC-12 / R3-FRUST-2 resolution

> Goal: a physics-comedy that *includes* everyone. Accessibility is also fun-design — assists keep solo/duo and mixed-skill crews in the laughter zone without stigma. Aligned to widely used game-accessibility guidance.

## 1. Input
- Full **remap** (gamepad + KB/M); **one-hand** layouts; **hold/toggle** for grab, ping, emote, sprint.
- **Sticky-grab assist** (raises hold tolerance); **auto-board** nearest station; **co-pilot assist** (light auto-balance) for solo/duo.
- Adjustable input deadzones & sensitivity; no required rapid mashing (alternatives provided).

## 2. Visual
- **Colorblind modes** (deuter/prot/trit) — hazards use **shape + color + motion**, never color alone.
- HUD scale & high-contrast HUD; objective/ping markers with optional outlines.
- **Photosensitivity:** reduce/disable flashes (lava, neon, sparks), cap flash frequency, dim strobe.
- Adjustable subtitle/caption size, background, and speaker labels.

## 3. Motion & camera (key for a physics game)
- Toggles/sliders for **screen shake, slow-mo (Disaster-Cam), camera-FOV, motion blur, head-bob**.
- "Reduce camera motion" preset; option to convert slow-mo to a still highlight for the motion-sensitive.

## 4. Audio
- Independent **Music/SFX/Voice/UI/Stinger** sliders; **mono** toggle; reduce-loud-sounds.
- **Captions** for VO + key SFX with directional indicators; a **visual strain meter** mirrors the audio creak→snap telegraph for hard-of-hearing players.

## 5. Cognitive & difficulty (no stigma)
- **Route choice** (safe/slow vs risky/fast) is the primary difficulty dial; **opt-in mutators** add challenge for points.
- **Hint level** (off / subtle / guided) and **slower-hazard** option.
- Clear, consistent iconography; non-blocking tutorials; pause in solo/Free Play.

## 6. Communication
- **Ping wheel** + **emote wheel** provide full no-voice coordination (text chat + quick-chat too).
- Per-player voice volume, mute, and push-to-talk; captions for teammates where supported.

## 7. Process
- Accessibility review each milestone; options surfaced in onboarding; tested with assistive setups in QA (doc 06). Default presets: Standard, Reduced-Motion, High-Contrast, One-Hand.
