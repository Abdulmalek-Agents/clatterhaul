## 3. Reasons to return (+4–6h, ongoing)
Daily Gauntlet · Foreman's Challenges (3 daily/3 weekly) · Endless personal bests · Perfect Hauls · Blueprint Garage + Workshop · Union Ranks + prestige · quarterly seasonal drops (doc 17).

## 4. Unlock cadence (carrot map; numbers in 26)
First 20 min: 1 module + 1 cosmetic. Every contract: a meaningful unlock choice. Every Boss-Haul: class-defining module / new biome. Rule: next unlock always visible & ≤ 1–2 hauls away (median time-to-unlock ≤ 20 min).

## 5. Difficulty & mastery
First Hill onboarding (doc 25); **safe/risky route forks** per leg; **opt-in mutators** multiply score (doc 23); mastery via Perfect Hauls/par/Daily/prestige; assists keep solo/duo & mixed-skill in the fun zone (docs 16/26).

## 6. Session shapes
15 min = 1 Daily; 30–45 min = 2–3 Contracts or 1 Endless; 60–90 min = a biome + Boss-Haul.

## 7. Launch inventory
34 hauls (doc 20) · 40 modules (21) · 12 cargo (22) · 16 mutators (23) · 60+ cosmetics · 8 emotes · 120+ barks (24) · Daily/Endless generators. All reuse one physics loop + asset-store art (cost thesis).
