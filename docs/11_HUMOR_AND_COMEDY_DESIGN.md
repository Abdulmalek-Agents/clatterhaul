# Clatterhaul — Humor & Comedy Design

**Version:** 2.0  ·  **Owner:** Comedy & Game Writers + Narrative + Animation  ·  Source of R1-FUN-3 / R2-HUMOR-2 resolution

> Thesis: **don't hope for laughs — engineer them.** Emergent physics gives us the raw material; these systems guarantee that *every session* lands authored and emergent comedy, and that the funniest moments are effortless to share.

## 1. Comedy pillars
1. **Funny, never mean.** Humor targets the *situation and the machine*, never a specific player. No insults; blame fate/weather/physics.
2. **Earnest underdogs.** The crew sincerely cares about the delivery; sincerity makes failure endearing, not nihilistic.
3. **Timing is a system.** Hit-stop, slow-mo, audio stingers, and beats are tuned for comedic rhythm (setup → anticipation → payoff).
4. **Shareable by default.** If it was funny, a clip already exists.
5. **Visual-first** (localizes everywhere — R3-LOC-7).

## 2. The comedy engine (systems that manufacture laughs)

| System | What it does | Why it's funny |
|---|---|---|
| **Active-ragdoll bonk** | players flail/tumble on impact, then recover | universal slapstick |
| **Expressive faces** | googly-eyed reaction faces (panic/triumph/dizzy) | reads emotion at a glance |
| **Emote wheel** | 8 emotes (point, cheer, facepalm, dance, "after you," cry, salute, shrug) | player-authored comedy + blame |
| **Voice barks** | context VO (grab, strain, win, fail) + proximity chat | timing + miscommunication gold |
| **Cargo personalities** | each cargo reacts (Reactor-Cat purrs/yowls, Chandelier tings, Ice-Sculpture sweats/melts, Fish sloshes) | the precious thing has feelings |
| **Disaster Cam** | auto slow-mo hero-shot on catastrophe | turns failure into spectacle |
| **Blooper Reel** | end-of-run montage of your 3 biggest disasters | the laugh payoff every session |
| **Gravestone Last-Words** | if the haul fails, a comedic epitaph card ("Here lies the cheese. It rolled with purpose.") | reframes loss as a punchline |
| **Foreman radio** | dry one-liners on depart/fail/triumph (doc 07) | authored wit between chaos |
| **Calamity staging** | telegraph→spike→resolve set-pieces (doc 13) | shared panic = shared laughter |
| **Nuisance fauna & prop gags** | goats, penguins, gators, pigeons; springy surfaces, catapult stands | unpredictable interruptions |

## 3. Comedic timing rules
- **Setup:** telegraph danger clearly (a creak, a wobble, a honking truck approaching).
- **Anticipation:** give 0.5–1.5s of "oh no" before the payoff (the funniest beat).
- **Payoff:** hit-stop + slow-mo + stinger on the disaster; then a quick recovery so play resumes (no dwell).
- **Callback:** Blooper Reel and gravestone reference the specific cargo/biome for personalized humor.

## 4. The Rule of Funny vs Fair
Comedy must never feel *cheap*. Every disaster traces to a readable cause (doc 10 strain telegraph) so players laugh *and* learn. Random unfair deaths are bugs, not jokes (QA "Laugh Audit" vs "Frustration Audit," doc 06).

## 5. Shareability pipeline (virality — R2-CLIP-4)
- Disasters auto-bookmark in **Replay Theater**.
- **One-click clip export** (last 15–30s) to MP4/GIF + Steam screenshot; optional auto-watermark.
- **Photo Mode** with filters, googly-eye toggle, and a "Clatter-cam" frame.
- End-of-run Blooper Reel has a "Save & Share" button.

## 6. Writing guidelines (for all VO/cards)
1. ≤ 12 words per bark; players talk over them.
2. Never name-blame a player; blame the rig/fate/weather.
3. Punch up at the absurdity, not down at anyone.
4. Every line should make a streamer want to read it aloud.
5. Keep it loc-friendly; avoid idioms; lead with the visual.
6. PG-13 ceiling; broadly shareable (no edgelord humor — R2-HUMOR anti-cringe).

## 7. Sample beats
- *Win:* Foreman: "Cargo intact?! Mark the calendar." + crew cheer emote auto-suggested.
- *Fail:* gravestone: "The chandelier saw things. Beautiful, fatal things."
- *Cargo gag:* Reactor-Cat yowls on a hard landing → crew flinch animation.
- *Calamity:* "Neon Rush Hour" — a hot-dog cart catapults a crew member into the shot during slow-mo.

## 8. Comedy QA (doc 06)
"Laugh Audit" each playtest: count genuine laughs/session (target ≥ 5), clip-worthy moments (≥ 2), and zero "that felt unfair" incidents. Tune systems toward the targets.
