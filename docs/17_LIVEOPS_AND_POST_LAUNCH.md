# Clatterhaul — Live-ops & Post-Launch Plan

**Version:** 2.0  ·  **Owner:** Live-ops Analyst + Game Directors + Community  ·  Source of R2-LIVE-13 resolution

> Friend-slop hits live or die on *momentum*. The plan: steady free content, community events, and creator fuel — monetized only by cosmetics — to keep the flywheel spinning and the review score climbing.

## 1. Cadence
| Beat | Frequency | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| **Hotfixes** | as needed | crash/perf/balance |
| **Content patches** | ~monthly | new mutators, cargo, cosmetics, Foreman's Challenges, balance |
| **Seasons** | ~quarterly | a new **free biome** (reuses systems), seasonal cosmetics, a community event, leaderboard reset |
| **Community Gauntlets** | weekly | curated Daily seeds + themed challenge + cosmetic reward |

## 2. Live systems already built-in (M6)
Daily Gauntlet, Foreman's Challenges, Union Ranks + prestige, leaderboards, telemetry, Steam Cloud, **Workshop blueprint sharing** — so live-ops is content + curation, not new tech.

## 3. Workshop & UGC
- Players publish/subscribe **contraption blueprints** and (post-launch) **paint/decal** sets.
- Curated "Foreman's Picks" weekly; featured blueprints earn the author a cosmetic.
- Moderation: report flow + automated validation (no broken/exploit rigs in featured).

## 4. Creator program (ties to marketing, doc 18)
- Creator keys, a built-in **clip export** + "Clatter-cam" frame, megaphone cosmetic, and seed-sharing for "play this exact disaster."
- Monthly clip contest (community-voted) with cosmetic prizes.

## 5. Monetization (post-launch)
- **Cosmetic-only** DLC packs (direct purchase, no gacha, no power). Seasonal free + premium cosmetic split.
- Optional charity/seasonal bundles. Never sell power or mutators.

## 6. Community management
- Official **Discord** (proximity-chat crowd lives here) + Steam forums + Reddit presence.
- Public roadmap, patch notes with personality (Foreman voice), and a feedback → backlog pipeline.
- Rapid response to review themes (the score is the currency).

## 7. KPIs
MAU/DAU & group-size mix, D1/D7/D30 retention, sessions/week, clip exports/shares, Workshop publishes/subs, Daily participation, review score trend, cosmetic attach-rate. Re-plan from data each season.

## 8. Post-launch roadmap sketch (subject to data)
- **S1:** new biome (e.g., Desert Highway), photo-mode frames, first community Gauntlet series.
- **S2:** new payload "characters" + cargo-reaction expansion, blueprint paint UGC.
- **S3:** cross-biome "Grand Haul" endless event + leaderboard season.
- **S4:** community-voted biome; anniversary cosmetics.
